Snippet24 Editorial & Legal Notes

  1. Snippet24 provides short AI-rephrased news summaries and links users to the original publisher for the complete report.
  2. Headlines, facts and source attribution should remain distinguishable from Snippet24's original editorial wording.
  3. Copyright ownership remains with the respective original publisher and rights holder.
  4. AI-generated summaries should not reproduce substantial portions of copyrighted articles.
  5. Legal, court and regulatory stories should be presented as reported developments and should not be represented as legal advice.
  6. Technology and AI coverage should distinguish reported facts from commentary, forecasts and AI-generated interpretation.
  7. Where a story concerns allegations, arrests, investigations or disputes, Snippet24 should avoid presenting allegations as established facts.
  8. Users should follow the original source link for the complete article, video, broadcast or official document.
